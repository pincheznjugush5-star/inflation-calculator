How the maths works

Converting between years

Value in year 2 equals the amount in year 1 multiplied by the ratio of their index values: amount × (index2 / index1). This is the same method the Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes.

Coverage varies

The United States series reaches back to 1850. Most other countries begin around 1960. Each country's index is national, and baskets differ between countries, so cross-country comparisons are approximate.

Projections

Future values compound your chosen rate annually from the last measured index value. Inflation is volatile, so a single rate is a simplifying assumption. Test a few rates to see the range of outcomes.

Data: World Bank (consumer price index, 190 countries) and, for the United States, the Bureau of Labor Statistics with Federal Reserve historical estimates. Figures before 1913 are estimates. Not financial advice.
